Privacy Policy

This application is a personal OAuth client used exclusively by its owner to access their own Google Drive through rclone.

Google user data

Google Drive data is accessed only for the purpose of allowing the user to view, create, modify, transfer, and manage their own files through rclone.

The application owner does not operate a separate server that collects, sells, shares, or distributes Google user data.

OAuth credentials and authorization tokens used by rclone are stored locally on the user's personal computer.

Data sharing

Google user data is not sold or shared with third parties by the application owner.
